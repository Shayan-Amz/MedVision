# MedVision & Clinical LLM Studio v4.0 — Scientific Limitations

1. **Research Prototype Scope (Non-Diagnostic):** MedVision AI Studio v4.0 is an academic research prototype and clinical decision-support (CDSS) proof-of-concept. It is not a cleared medical device and must not be used for autonomous clinical diagnosis or triage without prospective multi-center validation and regulatory review.
2. **External Weak-Label Feasibility (`IU-Xray`, `250` selected / `238` evaluated):** Binary labels are derived from report-impression heuristics and disagree with Open-I `Problems` annotations on `96/238 (40.34%, Cohen's kappa = 0.275)` (acute vs. chronic findings). Zero-event counts (`0/238` lexical contradictions and `0/238` frontal OOD false rejections) carry an exact Clopper-Pearson 95% CI of `[0.0%, 1.54%]`, not a zero population error rate.
3. **Operating-Point Trade-Offs:** The High-Specificity Alert Gate (`54.4%` Sens / `81.1%` Spec; `TP=43, FN=36, TN=129, FP=30`) misses `45.6%` of positive cases if used standalone and serves strictly as an anti-alert-fatigue pager escalation filter paired with higher-sensitivity clinician display (`ER Triage: 89.9%` Sens / `40.9%` Spec).
4. **Lexical Clinical Safety Filter Scope:** The Lexical Clinical Safety Filter is a rule-based post-processing component designed to detect and suppress a predefined subset of potentially hazardous clinical expressions in generated text. It is not a comprehensive semantic safety system, does not guarantee the detection of every potentially harmful recommendation, and does not replace qualified clinical judgment. Its evaluation is limited to the documented test cases and the specified external feasibility dataset.
5. **Disclosed Train-Bank RAG Provenance:** The `175,413`-passage FAISS index includes MedMCQA training-split explanations and clinical flashcards (`28.19%` gold option presence in Top-3 retrieved context; `51/4,183 = 1.22%` explicit Q&A key patterns). The `40` exact / `13` 8-gram (`47` union) lexical overlap audit covers Top-3 retrieved contexts and is not an exhaustive semantic deduplication of the full corpus. Furthermore, Cyclic-4 (`64.98%`) is not statistically superior to Single-Pass (`64.52%`, Exact McNemar `p = 0.356`).
6. **PubMedQA Class Imbalance & Vision Granularity:** PubMedQA `maybe` class F1 remains low (`13.79%`) despite `78.50%` accuracy and `60.05%` Macro-F1. In vision XAI, `256x256` classification limits millimeter-scale nodule localization (`2.5%` Pointing Game), and Montgomery manual lung masks exclude retrocardiac left-lung pixels (`83.26%` Left vs `90.36%` Right Dice).
