## 2. Statement of Purpose (SOP) Paragraph (Research-Focused)

> In my independent research project, *MedVision & Clinical LLM Studio*, I investigated how to build a reliable, resource-efficient modular multimodal clinical AI suite capable of running entirely on a single 12 GB consumer GPU without sacrificing methodological rigor. Enforcing strict patient-disjoint evaluation (`0` train/val/test overlap) across 103,213 NIH ChestX-ray14 radiographs (including 100% of the 25,596-image official test split), I achieved `0.8213` Mean AUROC on the 25,596-image official test split (Paired Bootstrap `B = 2,000`, `p < 0.001`, outperforming both individual backbones on all 14 pathologies), `64.98%` on full-split MedMCQA (`n = 4,183`, with a 175k-passage reranked Train-Bank RAG (documented Top-3 lexical overlap audit)), and `79.32%` Clinical Micro-F1 (`97.70%` negation specificity) on IU-Xray summarization. End-to-end validation on 250 selected (238 evaluated frontal) weak-label external studies from Indiana University (`IU-Xray`) confirmed 0 observed lexical contradictions in 238 evaluated cases, `100.0%` non-medical OOD rejection (`0.0%` frontal CXR false rejection), and 3-tier strict binary operating modes spanning `ER Triage` (`89.9%` Sens), `Balanced` (`74.7%` Sens / `69.2%` Spec), and `Alert Gate` (`81.1%` Spec)—all within `6.52 GB` VRAM and `2.53s` latency. This experience spanning statistical validation, clinical safety guardrails, and constrained-hardware systems optimization directly motivates my graduate research in trustworthy medical AI.
